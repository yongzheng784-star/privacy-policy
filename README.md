## Maintenance Notes

When advertising SDKs, ad networks, privacy disclosures, or Google Play store listing links change, update the relevant public files:

- `privacy_policy.html`
- `app-ads.txt`

After updating `app-ads.txt`, confirm that the final file is available at:

```text
https://yongzheng784-star.github.io/app-ads.txt
```

After updating the privacy policy, confirm that the final page is available at:

```text

```

Do not publish placeholder seller IDs such as `Publisher_ID` or `Business_ID`.
Do not add reseller lines for unused ad networks.
